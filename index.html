Learn More

Uttar Pradesh Jal Nigam (Urban) is committed to strengthening urban water supply and sewerage infrastructure across the state. Through quality engineering, effective project implementation, and sustainable infrastructure development, the Nigam works towards improving sanitation, supporting urban growth, and enhancing the quality of life for communities.

With a focus on reliable public services, modern technology, and responsible resource management, we strive to build cleaner, healthier, and more sustainable urban environments for the people of Uttar Pradesh.
